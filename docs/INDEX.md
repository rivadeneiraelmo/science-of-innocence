## Previously withheld — cleared and added October 24, 2026

- **K.R. scarf OCME exclusion reports (Pa1184–1190)** — initially withheld
  earlier on October 24, 2026 because the reports print the victim's full
  legal name while the rest of the record refers to victims by initials.
  Later that day Elmo expressly approved posting her full name, and the
  document was added to the set as `kr-scarf-exclusion-ocme.pdf` (see the
  main table above). Identifier scan before adding: the only
  personal-identifier fields in the range — the NYPD officers' Tax Registry
  numbers on Pa1190 and the OCME letterhead Tel/Fax/Email values on Pa1185
  and Pa1188 — were already blacked out in the as-filed scans themselves.
  No Social Security number, date of birth, personal phone number, home
  address, financial detail, or information about Elmo's family appears
  anywhere in Pa1184–1190, so no further redaction was required.

for the two court documents) for Social Security numbers, dates of birth,
personal phone numbers, home addresses, and victim full names:

  described, never named; no personal identifiers.
